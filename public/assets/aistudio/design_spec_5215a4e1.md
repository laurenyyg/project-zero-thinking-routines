## 1. Visual Aesthetics & Style Breakdown

### Strict Constraints Observed:
- **No Dark Background**: Clean, stark, gallery-grade pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **No Beige Background**: Zero cream, ecru, or beige tones.

### Three Artistic Influences:
1. **De Stijl (Mondrian & Van Doesburg)**:
   - High-contrast `2px`–`3px` solid black structural lines forming an asymmetrical, balanced orthogonal grid.
   - Pure primary color accents used strictly as functional markers (not gratuitous decoration).
2. **Bauhaus (Herbert Bayer, László Moholy-Nagy)**:
   - Form follows function.
   - Bold uppercase architectural tracking (`tracking-wider`, `font-bold`).
   - Clean geometric containers with micro or sharp corners (`rounded-none`).
3. **Suprematism (Malevich, El Lissitzky)**:
   - Pure elemental geometric shapes (■ square, ● circle, ▲ triangle) representing cognitive categories:
     - ■ **De Stijl Red Square**: *Synthesizing & Organizing*
     - ● **Cobalt Blue Circle**: *Digging for Truth & Evidence*
     - ▲ **Cadmium Yellow Triangle**: *Creativity & Decision Making*
     - ⬡ **Solid Black Diamond**: *Exploring Complexity & Systems*

## 2. Component Design Architecture

| Component | Visual Treatment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Top Navigation** | Bold black outline (`border-b-2 border-black`), solid black "PZ" monogram tile with red accent block, primary color tab indicators. |
| **AI Advisor (Chat)** | Modular white panels with 2px solid black frames, red left-accent rule for user messages, cobalt blue/yellow headers for AI recommendations, and sharp action buttons. |
| **Interactive Practice Cards** | Rectangular constructivist cards with primary color corner accents, high-contrast typography, and 1-click confirmation buttons. |
| **Practice Dashboard** | 4 modular metric tiles with primary shape emblems, flat solid color progress bars (Red/Blue/Yellow/Black) showing top routines used and category frequency. |
| **Routines Library** | Bounded De Stijl grid where each card feels like an architectural tile with category badge and practice counter. |
